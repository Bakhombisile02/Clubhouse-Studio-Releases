# Clubhouse Studio v1.3.6

Build 10. A patch update for authorized Clubhouse operators.

## Changes

- Adds NZSS CET Court 1, Court 7, and Court 8 automatically under Saved RTMP Codes on first launch. No manual entry is needed on each phone.
- Preserves destinations created in v1.3.5, last-used credentials, and current broadcast recovery data. The court presets are added once. Later edits and deletions remain in effect.
- Keeps the existing sports, scoreboards, camera, recording, and streaming controls. No dependency upgrades or remote-control changes are included.

## Before updating

Update between broadcasts. Install the signed APK over the existing app without uninstalling it.

If you upgrade directly from v1.3.4 or earlier, the previously announced v1.3.5 retirement still clears old destinations and credential-bearing shortcuts before adding the three CET presets. Keep any still-needed older credentials in your approved secure records first.

## Validation

The signed APK passed an in-place upgrade from v1.3.5 on a physical Samsung Galaxy S25. All three courts appeared automatically, each selection showed configured and hidden credentials, and the entries persisted after restart. An existing test destination survived the upgrade and was then removed. Local recording, score correction, clock controls, fullscreen, and confirmed recording end were exercised. The resulting 1080p recording contains video and audio and decoded successfully.

All six required CI checks passed, including JVM tests, lint, build, workflow policy, secret scanning, and signature/attribution checks. APK version, build number, approved signer, exact destination catalogue, and checksum were verified. This is a targeted patch smoke test, not a full app regression or a camera picture-quality assessment.

No external RTMP connection, handshake, or broadcast was performed. Event availability and permission to publish must be confirmed with the event lead.

## Installation

Download `CLUBHOUSE-v1.3.6.apk` and compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. An in-place update requires the same signing identity. Play-installed builds may use a different signer. Play Protect makes its own trust decisions.

The AAB remains private. Remote Scoring is unavailable.
